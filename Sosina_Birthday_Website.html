One last page
10 / a note from your person

Dear Sosina,

Happy birthday, my dear friend.

I hope this year gives you room to become even more yourself. I hope you find work that makes you curious, people who see the value in the way you think and opportunities that stretch your imagination in the best possible ways.

You spend so much time thinking about how places are built, how people move through them and how a city can make life better. I hope you get to build a life with that same care: one with room for discovery, meaningful work, unexpected adventures, quiet days and the people who make you feel at home.

This little atlas is a reminder that your degree is not a narrow road. It can lead to research, climate work, technology, public policy, design, finance, humanitarian work and paths that do not have names yet. You do not have to choose them all. You only have to stay open to the ones that feel like yours.

And when the future feels too big, remember you can take it one small step at a time. You are allowed to change direction. You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to surprise yourself.

May the year ahead be kind to you, brave with you and full of beautiful possibilities.

With so much love,
Your best friend
